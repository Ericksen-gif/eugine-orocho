WHO WE ARE

Transforming Real Estate Through Expertise & Innovation

Great Lansba Realtors is a Nairobi-based property management and real estate services company dedicated to delivering professional, innovative and client-focused property solutions.

Founded by industry veteran Eugene Orocho, the company brings together over 17 years of combined expertise and a deep understanding of the Kenyan real estate market.

We combine professional property management, data-driven insights and strategic marketing to help property owners, investors and businesses achieve better results from their real estate assets.

Our approach is designed to address some of the key challenges facing property owners, including rent arrears, tenant turnover, maintenance and property performance.
